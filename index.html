What is Verification?

Verification vs. Validation: Validation proves a new or modified assay works. Verification proves that an unmodified, FDA-cleared instrument performs according to manufacturer specifications in your lab.

CLIA Requirements: Before bringing an instrument online, you must verify Accuracy, Precision, Reportable Range (AMR), and Reference Intervals.
